Design the PCB for a small environmental sensor board.

- An ATmega328P microcontroller runs at 3.3 V from an 8 MHz crystal.
- It reads temperature, humidity and pressure from a Bosch BME280 sensor
  over I2C.
- Power enters on a 2-pin 2.54 mm header: pin 1 is 3.3 V, pin 2 is ground.

Choose component values from the parts' datasheets. Deliver a fab-ready board.
