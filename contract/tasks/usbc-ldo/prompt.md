Design the PCB for a small USB-C power board.

- Power comes in on a USB-C receptacle, from an ordinary USB-C charger or a
  laptop's USB-C port.
- The board supplies a regulated 3.3 V output at up to 500 mA on a 2-pin
  2.54 mm header: pin 1 is 3.3 V, pin 2 is ground.
- Regulate with a linear regulator (an LDO).

Choose component values from the parts' datasheets. Deliver a fab-ready board.
